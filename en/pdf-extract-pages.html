Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages at once?

Yes. You can select scattered pages like 1, 3, and 7 together, and they will be saved in their original order in the new PDF.

How is this different from deleting pages?

Deleting pages removes the ones you select and keeps the rest. Extracting pages saves only the ones you select into a new PDF — more convenient when you only need a few pages.

Can I preview pages before extracting?

Yes. Page thumbnails are shown so you can select the exact pages to extract.
