Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between grayscale and dark mode?

Grayscale converts colors to gray to save color ink when printing. Dark mode inverts the page — dark background, light text — for comfortable reading in low light on screen.

Does grayscale really save printer ink?

Yes. Color elements become gray, reducing color cartridge use. The effect is large when printing lots of material or papers.

Can I still search and copy the text?

Depending on the conversion, the text layer is sometimes kept and sometimes rasterized. Keep the original if search matters.

Can a dark-mode PDF be reverted?

The converted file itself cannot be reverted. Your original stays intact, so treat the dark-mode copy as a reading copy.

Can I convert image-heavy PDFs to grayscale?

Yes. Photos and charts are converted to gray too. Low-contrast images may look faint in grayscale.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely inside your browser and your files are never sent to a server. There is no risk of personal or sensitive data leaving your device, so it is safe to use even in a corporate environment.
