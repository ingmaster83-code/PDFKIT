Frequently Asked Questions

What is N-up used for?

It places several pages onto one sheet — 2, 4, or 6 up — to make a print-ready PDF. It saves paper and ink and is used for handouts, booklets, and handout slides.

What if the page count doesn't divide evenly?

The leftover slots on the last sheet are blank. For example, 9 pages at 4-up leaves one page on the last sheet.

Can I choose row-first or column-first order?

Pages are generally placed left to right, top to bottom — suitable for documents where reading order matters, like lecture notes or scripts.

Can I still search text after N-up?

Scaling the pages down may rasterize the text, so search may not work. Keep the original.

Can I just view it on screen instead of printing?

Yes. It's also handy for skimming several pages at once to review the overall flow quickly.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely inside your browser and your files are never sent to a server. There is no risk of personal or sensitive data leaving your device, so it is safe to use even in a corporate environment.
