Frequently Asked Questions

Can text under the black box still be copied or extracted later?

No. This tool doesn't just cover with a box — it rebuilds the page as an image and removes the original text itself. Copying or text extraction will not reveal the hidden content.

Is it suitable for hiding ID numbers or account numbers?

Yes. It is built for permanently removing sensitive information before submitting or sharing documents that contain personal data.

What happens to pages where I didn't draw a box?

Pages without redaction marks are also converted to images and saved. Text search and copy across the whole document will be gone.

Can I undo a redaction I made by mistake?

Before saving you can move or delete the boxes, but after saving it cannot be recovered. Always keep the original separately.

The file got larger after redacting.

Turning pages into images can increase the size. If there's a submission limit, reduce it with the compression tool.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely inside your browser and your files are never sent to a server. There is no risk of personal or sensitive data leaving your device, so it is safe to use even in a corporate environment.
