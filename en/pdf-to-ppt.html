Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit the PDF text directly in PowerPoint?

No. Each PDF page is converted into a single image and placed on a slide. To edit the actual text, use the PDF to Word converter instead.

Is there a limit on the number of pages?

There is no page limit. However, more pages will take longer to convert and produce a larger file, so Medium quality is recommended over High for large documents.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser and files are never sent to a server. There's no risk of personal or sensitive data leaking out, so it's safe to use even in enterprise security environments.
