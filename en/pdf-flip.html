💡 Tips

  • Rotate (90° turns) and Flip (mirror) are different — use Flip when a scan came out mirror-reversed.
  • Applies identically to every page.
  • Keep the original file if you'll need to edit or search the text later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from rotate?

Rotate turns the page 90 degrees clockwise. Flip mirrors it horizontally or vertically, so text appears reversed left-right or upside-down like a mirror image.

Can I still edit or search the text?

The page is converted to an image during flipping, so text selection and search won't work in the result. It's a visual flip only.

Are files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser and files are never sent to a server.
